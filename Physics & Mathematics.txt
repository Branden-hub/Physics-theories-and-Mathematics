Physics & Mathematics:

String Theory – A candidate for the theory of everything, describing reality in terms of vibrating strings.

Loop Quantum Gravity – An approach to quantum gravity that suggests spacetime itself is quantized.

Holographic Principle – The idea that all information about a volume of space is encoded on its boundary.

Mathematical Universe Hypothesis – The proposal that reality itself is a mathematical structure.

Computational & Simulation-Based Approaches:

Digital Physics – The concept that the universe operates like a computational system.

Simulation Hypothesis – The idea that reality might be an artificial simulation.

Cellular Automata – Simple computational rules generating complex behaviors, possibly mimicking physical laws.

Agent-Based Modeling – A framework for simulating reality using autonomous entities that learn and adapt.

Complexity & Emergence:

Complex Systems Theory – The study of emergent behaviors arising from interactions of simple components.

Network Science – The analysis of structural connections at multiple scales, from neurons to galaxies.

Fractal Cosmology – The idea that the structure of the universe exhibits self-similar patterns across scales.

Emergence Theory – Explains how higher-level phenomena arise that cannot be reduced to their parts.

Consciousness & Reality Perception:

Integrated Information Theory (IIT) – A mathematical framework for understanding consciousness.

Quantum Consciousness – The hypothesis that quantum effects contribute to conscious experience.

Predictive Processing – A model in which reality emerges through the brain’s updating of predictions based on sensory input.

Embodied Cognition – The idea that consciousness is shaped by sensorimotor interactions with the environment.

Machine Consciousness & AI Applications:

Computational Theory of Mind – A framework for implementing consciousness-like processes in artificial systems.
