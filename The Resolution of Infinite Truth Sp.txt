Let $\phi : \mathcal{A} \times D \to \mathbb{R}$ be a \emph{Phase Alignment} functional, and let $\gamma \in \mathbb{R}$ be a resonance threshold.

Define the \emph{resonant mutation set}:


\[
\mathcal{M}_\gamma(S)
=
\bigl\{ S' \in \mathcal{M}(S) : \phi(S', D) \ge \gamma \bigr\}.
\]



The phase-lock update rule is:


\[
S_{t+1}
=
\operatorname*{arg\,min}_{S' \in \mathcal{M}_\gamma(S_t)}
\mathcal{L}_{ZR}(S' \mid D).
\]



\subsection*{VII. Global Optimization Problem}

The Zero-Redundancy Extraction Algorithm seeks


\[
S^\*
=
\operatorname*{arg\,min}_{S \in \mathcal{A}}
\mathcal{L}_{ZR}(S \mid D).
\]



\subsection*{VIII. Zero-Redundancy Convergence Identity}

Let $H(D)$ denote the empirical entropy of the dataset $D$. Under ideal conditions (perfect model class and infinite computation), ZREA converges to a generator $S^\*$ such that


\[
H(D) = K(S^\*),
\]


i.e.,


\[
\Psi_C = S^\* \quad \text{and} \quad H(\Psi_{\dagger}) = K(\Psi_C).
\]



This expresses the Zero-Redundancy principle: all compressible structure in the Collapsed State is captured by the minimal-complexity Coherent State.
